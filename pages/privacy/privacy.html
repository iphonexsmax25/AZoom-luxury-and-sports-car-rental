Your Personal Data, Your Rights

AZoom complies with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). We collect only the personal data we need to rent you a car, we keep it secure, and you can ask to access, correct or withdraw consent for your data at any time. Questions? Contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@azoom.sg.

1. Introduction & Our PDPA Commitment

AZoom (“the Company”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect your personal data when you use our website and rent vehicles from us, in line with Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (“PDPA”).

“Personal data” means data about an individual who can be identified from that data, or from that data and other information we have or are likely to have access to. This policy applies to renters, website visitors and anyone else whose personal data we handle.

By creating an account, making a booking or otherwise giving us your personal data, you confirm that you have read this policy and consent to the collection, use and disclosure of your personal data as described here.

2. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect the following:

  • Account data: your email address and an encrypted form of your password.
  • Driver and contact details: full name, phone number, email address and driving licence number.
  • Booking data: the vehicle you rent, pickup and return locations, rental dates, price paid and booking and receipt numbers.
  • Payment data: the name on your card, and the last 4 digits of your card. We do not store your full card number, expiry date or CVC.
  • Inspection records: reports on the condition of the vehicle when it is returned, linked to your booking, including any damage found and any damage charge.
  • Communications: messages you send to our support team.

3. How We Collect Your Personal Data

  • Directly from you, when you register an account, complete the booking and payment forms, or contact us.
  • From your use of our services, for example your bookings, returns and payments.
  • From our employees, when they approve a booking, record the return of a vehicle or complete an inspection.
  • From third parties where you have authorised them, or where the law permits.

4. Purposes of Collection, Use & Disclosure

We collect, use and disclose your personal data only for purposes that a reasonable person would consider appropriate in the circumstances, and for which you have been notified or have given consent. These purposes include:

  • Creating and managing your account.
  • Verifying that you are eligible to drive and processing your booking and payment.
  • Preparing, handing over and inspecting the vehicle, and recording its condition.
  • Charging damage costs, late fees, fines and other charges that are due under our Terms & Conditions.
  • Sending booking confirmations, receipts and service messages.
  • Responding to your enquiries, feedback and complaints.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, misuse and security incidents.
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations, and enforcing our rights.

We will not use your personal data for a new purpose without notifying you and, where the PDPA requires it, obtaining your consent first.

5. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only in these situations:

  • With payment service providers, to process your payments.
  • With service providers who help us run our business, such as hosting, support and repair services, who may only use the data for the services they provide to us and are required to protect it.
  • With insurers and repairers where a claim or repair is involved.
  • With the police, traffic authorities, courts and other government bodies where we are required or permitted by law, for example to identify the driver for a traffic offence, parking fine or ERP charge.
  • With a buyer or successor if our business is sold or restructured, subject to the same level of protection.

6. Payment Information

Card payments are encrypted and processed securely. We keep only the last 4 digits of your card number, so that you can recognise the card on your booking and receipt. Your full card number, expiry date and CVC are not stored by us.

7. Cookies & Browser Storage

Our website uses your browser’s storage to keep you signed in and to remember your bookings. This storage stays in your own browser. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings, but you may then need to sign in again and some features may not work as expected.

We do not use browser storage to track you across other websites.

8. Protection & Security of Personal Data

We take reasonable security steps to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, modification, disposal or loss. These include:

  • Storing passwords in an encrypted (hashed) form, never in plain text.
  • Limiting access to bookings, driver details and inspection reports to employees who need them to do their work.
  • Keeping inspection reports only in controlled folders.
  • Training our staff to handle personal data carefully.

No system is completely secure. Please keep your password private and sign out after using a shared device.

9. Retention of Personal Data

We keep your personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes it was collected for, or for legal or business purposes. Booking, payment and inspection records are generally kept for up to 5 years, to meet our tax, accounting and legal obligations and to handle any claims or disputes. When we no longer need the data, we delete it or remove the means by which it can be linked to you.

10. Transfers Outside Singapore

If we need to transfer your personal data outside Singapore, for example to a cloud service provider, we will make sure that the recipient provides a standard of protection comparable to that required under the PDPA, in line with the PDPA’s Transfer Limitation Obligation.

11. Your Rights: Access, Correction & Withdrawal of Consent

Under the PDPA you have the following rights:

  • Access: you may ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and for information on how it has been used or disclosed in the past year. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible, and in any case within 30 days. We may charge a reasonable fee for the request and will tell you the amount first.
  • Correction: you may ask us to correct an error or omission in your personal data. We will correct it as soon as practicable, usually within 30 days.
  • Withdrawal of consent: you may withdraw your consent to our collection, use or disclosure of your personal data at any time by giving us reasonable notice. We will tell you what the likely consequences are. For example, if you withdraw consent we need to rent you a car, we may no longer be able to provide the service. Withdrawing consent does not affect data we are allowed to keep by law.
  • Marketing: you may opt out of marketing messages at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us. We check the Do Not Call Registry before sending any marketing by phone call or text message.

To make a request, contact our Data Protection Officer using the details below. We may need to verify your identity first.

12. Data Breach Notification

If a data breach occurs, we will assess it promptly. If it is likely to cause you significant harm, or is of a significant scale, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) as soon as practicable and no later than 3 calendar days after we assess it to be notifiable. Where the breach is likely to cause you significant harm, we will also notify you so that you can take steps to protect yourself.

13. Accuracy of Your Information

We rely on the information you give us to be accurate and complete, for example your name, phone number and driving licence number. Please keep it up to date and tell us if anything changes. Giving us false information may result in your booking being cancelled, as set out in our Terms & Conditions.

14. Data Protection Officer & Contact Us

We have appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO) who is responsible for making sure we comply with the PDPA. If you have a question, a complaint, or a request relating to your personal data, please contact:

  • Data Protection Officer, AZoom
  • Email: dpo@azoom.sg
  • Support: use the Contact section on our website

We will respond to your enquiry or complaint promptly. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) at www.pdpc.gov.sg.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. We will notify you of material changes where required, and your continued use of our services after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Questions About Your Data?

Our Data Protection Officer can help with access and correction requests, consent withdrawal and any other privacy concern. You can also read our Terms & Conditions.
